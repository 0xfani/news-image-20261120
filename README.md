One picture of Friday 20 November 2026: the day's world news on a single 1024×1024 front page. A big dated masthead, a red standfirst naming the leads, region and field bars, the day in numbers, 15 illustrated story cards (3 leads + 12) with minimal flat pictograms and per-card source labels, a world map with numbered pins, 33 numbered "Also on" briefs (48 stories in all, 7 regions, 7 fields), and a green date-check box stating that every story happened or was first reported on 20 November 2026.

Stories were gathered from the RSS feeds of BBC, Guardian, NYT, Al Jazeera, France 24, NBC, Japan Times, Straits Times, Times of India, Anadolu, ABC and RNZ, keeping only items first published on 20 November in UTC. Stories already reported on 19 November (the Trump–Xi–Putin summit, the World Court's Myanmar ruling, Voyager 1's light-day milestone) are left out and named in the date-check box.

The repository holds the finished page as `entry.png`; `main.py` copies it to the output folder. No network, no randomness: every run writes identical bytes.

## Run it

```sh
python main.py   # writes entry.png to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR, or /output in the make.fun sandbox
```
